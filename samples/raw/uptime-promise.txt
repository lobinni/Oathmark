ACME DEMO SERVICE — SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENT
Effective date: published version of this file
Document: public uptime promise for manual testing

SCOPE
This commitment applies to the Acme demo service production environment
and to every customer with an active subscription.

UPTIME PROMISE
The service maintains at least 99 percent monthly uptime.

Uptime is measured per calendar month across all production regions.
A month in which the service is unreachable for more than 0.9 percent of
the minutes in that month qualifies the customer for a service credit of
one full month of subscription fees, claimable through support.

MEASUREMENT
Availability is measured from at least three independent monitoring
regions at five-minute intervals. A failed check in two or more regions
at the same time counts as five minutes of unavailability.

MAINTENANCE
Scheduled maintenance windows are announced at least 72 hours in advance
through the status page and count against the same monthly budget. No
maintenance window may exceed four hours.

REPORTING
Monthly uptime reports are published within the first five business days
of the following month and list every qualifying unavailability period.

This document is the complete and authoritative statement of the uptime
commitment for the demo service. No other document amends it.
