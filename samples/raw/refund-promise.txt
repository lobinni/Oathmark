ACME DEMO SERVICE — REFUND AND SUPPORT COMMITMENTS
Effective date: published version of this file
Document: public customer protection policy for manual testing

REFUND COMMITMENT
Customers may cancel within thirty days and receive a full refund.

The thirty-day window starts on the day of the initial purchase. Refunds
are returned to the original payment method within ten business days and
require no justification from the customer.

SUPPORT RESPONSE COMMITMENT
Support requests receive a first response within one business day.

A first response is a substantive reply from a support engineer, not an
automated acknowledgement. Business days exclude weekends and public
holidays in the customer's region.

HOW TO CLAIM
Send a message to the support channel listed in the account dashboard and
reference your order number. Both commitments above apply automatically;
no separate registration is required.

This document is the complete and authoritative statement of the refund
and support commitments for the demo service. No other document amends it.
